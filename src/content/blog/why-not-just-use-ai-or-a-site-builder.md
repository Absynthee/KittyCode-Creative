It's a fair question, and one worth asking before you spend any money. You can ask an AI to build you a website in an afternoon, or sign up to a site builder for a few pounds a month. So why pay someone to do it for you?

## AI can make a page that looks good

AI is very good at producing something that looks the part. Give it a prompt and you'll get a page with a hero, a few sections and a colour scheme that hangs together.

This is good for a visually pleasing landing page - but what you won't get is a site that performs. Looking good and working well are two different problems, and it's the second one that costs you customers. AI doesn't know your business, your customers, or what you need someone to actually do once they land on the page. It answers the prompt in front of it, not the goals behind it.

It also can't test anything. It cannot open your site on a five year old Android phone, or on a tablet held sideways, or with a screen reader, or at 200% zoom. These aren't edge cases - they're a serious chunk of real visitors, and they're exactly where generated code tends to come apart.

Then there's everything that isn't code. Vibe coding hands you the files and stops. It doesn't buy your domain, set up your hosting, sort your email, keep the site online, or fix it at 9pm on a Friday when something breaks. All of that would still be yours to work out.

## Site builders work, if you have the time

Site builders are an option if you have the hours and the patience. It's still a lot of work, but you can get a working site out of one.

The catch is the hours. It's a proper time commitment, and it's time taken away from running your business. Plenty of people start one, get most of the way through, and leave it sitting half-finished for months because something more urgent came up.

The other catch is performance. Sites built this way tend to carry a lot of weight they never use, which slows them down, and a slow site both ranks worse and loses visitors before the page has finished loading. You also get limited control over the details search engines and AI assistants read to work out what your business does and whether to recommend it.
